Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start learning?

Open Vocab Studio, choose your display and translation languages, select a vocabulary topic, and then choose a learning mode such as Flashcards, Loop Show, eBook, Matching, or Quiz.

Can I use Vocab Studio offline?

Yes. Download the available vocabulary content while connected to the internet. Downloaded words, images, and audio can then be used offline.

How do I download or update content?

Open the content or topic section and select the available download or update option. Keep the app open and maintain a stable internet connection until the download finishes.

Why can't I hear pronunciation audio?
  • Make sure your device volume is turned up.
  • Check that sound is enabled in Vocab Studio.
  • Confirm that the required content has downloaded.
  • Restart the app and try playing the word again.
How do I change the display language?

Open Settings in Vocab Studio. You can independently select the app interface language and the language used for word translations.

What does Vocab Studio Premium include?

Premium unlocks all available Premium vocabulary topics, words, images, pronunciation audio, and future Premium topic collections. Current subscription options and prices are displayed in the app before purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings or the Premium screen and select Restore Purchases. Make sure your device is signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
  1. Open the Settings app on your Apple device.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Vocab Studio.
  5. Choose the available management or cancellation option.

Deleting Vocab Studio does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

How is learning progress stored?

Vocab Studio records learning activity such as reviewed words, quiz performance, learning streaks, and topic progress on your device.
